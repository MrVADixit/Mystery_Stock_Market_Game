# Participant guide

Everything you need, on one page. You do not need to read it before playing.

---

## Getting in

1. Open the link your teacher gives you, or go to `/join` and type the game code.
2. Pick a display name and a passphrase.
3. That is it.

**Remember your passphrase.** If your phone loses signal, drops the page or dies,
you get back in with the same name and passphrase and everything — your cash,
your shares, your purchases — is exactly where you left it. Nothing is stored on
your device.

---

## The main screen

At the top: which round it is and how long is left.

```
ROUND 07 / 24          TIME REMAINING 04:38
```

Below that, your money:

```
TOTAL ASSETS          112,450 WON
AVAILABLE CASH         32,450 WON
STOCK HOLDINGS VALUE   80,000 WON
```

Then the seven stocks, each showing its price, how it moved last round and how
many shares you hold.

---

## Buying and selling

1. Set the quantity with **−** and **+**.
2. Press **BUY** or **SELL**.

That is the whole interaction. There is no confirmation step and no checkout.
You will see a spinner, then a tick, and your balances update.

- **MAX BUY** — buy as many as your cash allows.
- **ALL** — sell your entire holding of that stock.
- In **View portfolio** you can sell any single holding, or everything at once
  (that one asks you to confirm first).

You cannot spend money you do not have or sell shares you do not hold; the
buttons go grey rather than letting you try. If you tap twice quickly, only one
order is placed.

---

## The four buttons

**Market graphs** — every stock's price history. Tap one for a full-screen chart;
turn your phone sideways for a bigger view. There is also a comparison view for
putting several stocks on the same axes.

**Rules & intel** — what is known about each stock's hidden rule. Four slots per
stock: purple means public, green means you bought it privately, grey means
nobody knows yet. Tap a stock to read what you have.

**Leaderboard** — the standings, and how each person's rank has moved.

**Impose a secret** — spend your secret token.

---

## Secrets

You have one token. Spend it on a stock and that stock's price, trading activity
and holdings are hidden from everyone else for two rounds, starting next round.

- Nobody is told it was you.
- Everyone can see that *some* stock is concealed.
- When it expires, the hidden rounds become visible again.
- You can impose one up to the end of round 20.

When a stock is concealed you will see `•••••` instead of its price.

---

## Intelligence

Each stock's rule is revealed in four stages, at rounds 5, 10, 15 and 20. Round
20 reveals everything.

You start with one **Piece**. Spend it on a stock to get that stock's next
fragment privately, ahead of everybody. You get the next one in the sequence —
you cannot pick which.

Or exchange the Piece for 10,000 won instead. One clue, or ten percent more
capital. Both are reasonable; they are not equally good in every game.

---

## Four things worth knowing

**Prices only move between rounds.** Nothing you do changes a price inside the
round you do it in. Trade for the *next* price, not this one.

**Nothing falls below 100 won.** A stock on the floor can come back up just as
fast as it went down.

**Watch the whole market, not just your stocks.** At least one stock is driven by
another stock, and at least one is driven by how the market as a whole moved. The
connections are where the money is.

**One stock ignores you completely.** Exactly one of the seven follows a path
decided before the game started. No amount of buying, selling or secrecy touches
it. Working out which one saves you a lot of wasted effort.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **The page looks stuck.** It refreshes by itself every few seconds; pull down
  to reload if you like. A small amber dot near the countdown means it is
  reconnecting.
- **You lost your session.** Go back to `/join` and sign in with the same name
  and passphrase.
- **An order was refused.** The message says why — usually not enough cash, not
  enough shares, or the round closed while you were tapping.
